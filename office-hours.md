## How to Use Office Hours

You are welcome to attend office hours even if you do not have a fully formed question.

Good reasons to come:

- You are stuck on homework.
- You want to review a lecture concept.
- You want advice on studying for exams.
- You want feedback on your solution-writing.
- You missed part of a lecture and need help catching up.
